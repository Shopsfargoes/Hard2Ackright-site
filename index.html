How To Use

Built into your warm-up,
not your whole practice.

A few reps, consistently, beats an overhaul of your whole workout. Use HARD2ACKRIGHT at the start of your session — here's where to start.

Three-Man Weave

Run the standard weave up and down the floor, but swap in HARD2ACKRIGHT for the passes. The added weight forces cleaner mechanics — you'll feel exactly where your passing form breaks down under load.

Two-Ball Dribbling

Pair HARD2ACKRIGHT with a regular ball and dribble simultaneously. The contrast in weight and bounce sharpens control in both hands and builds a feel for handling anything that comes off a defender's hands.

Wall Passes

Rapid-fire chest and bounce passes against a wall. The resistance builds passing strength fast, and the quick reset between reps trains your hands to recover under fatigue.
